Maybe We Glimmer

If there ever came a day when forests could be owned, Soren and Kishi would absolutely get immediate authority over this nook. They came here once by a happy accident as young adults while they were running away after stealing 2 loaves of honey bread from their society's only shop. The news would have needed no fuel to spread given how tiny the society was. But they made it. They ran until they mutually decided that neither their nor the other's body could keep running anymore.

That was, however, 13 years ago. They did not need to run anymore. The nook was constant. It would always have been there no matter how many times they went. They could enter the forest from any place and by sound or trees, or soil patterns, they would always end up there perfectly. If nothing worked, they simply needed to walk and by the change in temperature, the cool and calm it pulsated, to which only they were versed- would bring them in regardless. This was also why they never understood why no one else ever came here, for it was such a spectacular spot.

They sit down on 'their' log. It was covered by a razor-thin spread of moss, on which 4 a.m. dew had settled gently and perfectly. It was not winter yet but their cheeks would go cold soon anyways. Weird how utterly silent it felt at that moment. The little pond stood reverently still as if moving would be a grievous crime. The trees sleeping on the shoulders of other trees next to them; their leaves visibly relaxed and falling. It was almost as if looking too long at the leaves could make you sleep as well.

"You do realize that we are grown ups now and have better options than whatever this is...whatever you are suggesting... right?" whispered Soren. His eyes velvet red from being rubbed and made to cry again immediately after they had just stopped. He had a sob the size of an apple stuck in his throat. His next words, he did not know himself, but knew enough to realize that whatever they may be, would definitely lodge the apple deeper; and so each time he spoke, it was vehemently calculated, or at least as best as he could... for he could barely think.

"I do realize that. You know I do. But it has to be this way... you know it...it has been decreed" whispered back Kishi at the same volume, but even more gently. Her tenderness came with a note which pricked Soren each time she spoke because he felt miserable at how peaceful she appeared... or at least seemed to be.

"You know all too well that the order is fake, unreal, and carries no weight, hell, the Regent knows himself that it is way too early to even pass such an order, if it was ever in his authority to do so in the first place!" softly barked Soren. The apple lodged itself deeper.

"I am aware, yes."

"Then why must you? It just does not make sense to me... I do not see reason in no matter how many times I look or- or- or however many ways I employ..."

"It has to be this way, maybe you're not looking correctly, or maybe you should have a discourse with yourself about your idea that you deem yourself fit for an explanation at all?"

"As if you have an explanation for yourself Kishi..."

"I don't... and we are both aware of the fact that I don't "

"What reason do YOU see then? What causes YOU to believe it then?"

"Because it has to be this way Soren. Or- I dont know. If ultimately you are right, well then it will be a victory most grand for the both of us and it will seem like none of what we worried about in these moments mattered. But if that is not to be true- then our thoughts, stress and the questions posed at us and the best of our answers we can prepare for then in our lifetimes will be worth it too- but as a negative cost. Who decides what is positive and what is negative anyways, Soren. A useless brick today is a tool that keeps furniture lifted just the right way in the future. Misery does not call for reason most of the time. I would however, kill to lay down right here right now on this patch of grass-" said Kishi as she pointed down with her finger towards an inviting part of the ground covered in soft grass that ran endlessly throughout the forest. When she looked down at it, the tears in her own eyes parted ways with her and fell upon her thighs. "- that is all I can think of for myself"

Something snapped in the grave silence. Oh it was just a twig. Turns out there are only so many questions you are allowed to ask in peace regardless of whether you get the answer or not, before the universe deems it fit that you may be stunned or startled by something, just to remind you of your place. Soren cannot be allowed reason and so it must only be reasonable that he's not allowed to question either.

He looked back at the direction of the sharp sound. The trees and their leaves were awake. Unfortunately for him, each one of them was staring at Soren. He turned back to see the Sun glistening in the morning sky.

He could see Kishi walking away, deeper into the woods... or out? His apple had become 2 so he dared not speak. He would be punished- he knew, so he dared not speak. He would have to sit with questions unanswered, and stripped of his silence as well if he poked at it, so he dared not speak.

What he could do was enjoy the moss and how soft it felt. Maybe that was life.

Maybe that was life?

He looked up, his nose struggling to draw air, his head about to fall and take his body with it. He saw the pond, softly crashing at the edges as the sun's light glimmered and sparkled on the little waves and glittered yellow.

Soren picked up a rock and hurled it at the little pond; the rock whistled in its path to it and smashed the serene water with not so much of a splash as much as it was a violent thud. Stray drops of cold water found their way to his body. With a coarse throat he managed to gargle out the words-

"How dare you?"

It took 3 more seconds for Soren to realize he could not breathe anymore. And 3 more to realize that he would rather have it this way. After all, how dare he challenge the pond?
