Hey Luna!

When I stay out on my roof for a little too long at night, the idea of an incoming call by my dad asking me to come back downstairs, paired with "What were you doing?", starts finding its way gradually up my spine. It's like someone holding your shoulder, but their hands are cold and their grip tightening every second, forcing you to acknowledge it at one point regardless of your disregard towards it. But yesterday at night, when it took longer than usual for the call to arrive, I realized that I might have more time than I imagined. So I continued staring at the moon.

Upon doing so, the thought that often comes to my mind revisited me. It was the idea that the moon I am looking at right now is the same moon which was seen, stared at, and gazed upon by every single human and almost every living thing with eyes. Yes, I realize that conjuring this thought does not take a lot of pondering. It is pretty obvious when it hits you. But like soda hitting the nasal pathway a few minutes after drinking it is a strong reaction, the bigger and heavier realization lies in what that truly even means.

Imagine being 4.46 billion years old. And while that amount of time simply cannot be processed by the human brain, so I might just say 1 billion, or 10 billion, or 100 billion, know that it does not mean much to the human brain. We were not built to wrap our minds around such huge stretches of time. We can simply file it under "a lot" for now. Imagine spinning around a blue marble and knowing how each and every life form was born; how each Neanderthal looked up at you, without even knowing what you are at all, how each kingdom rose and fell, how each lover looked up to you and placed a wish upon you, even though you could not have done much, how each child imagined you to be their grandparent of some sort.

At this point, I like to think that the moon is this huge rock, but also a titanic mailbox. Billions- no, countless- wishes and hopes, prayers and requests, sent to you. Must be tiring for you, isn't it? Pretty sure you did try, at some point, to somehow make them work; threw a fit because you could not do anything; had a tantrum because you were young at some point too. Maybe as you got older you did get some powers to fulfill some of them. Or maybe you still take them, but keep them tucked away, with a tender heart, knowing you cannot fulfill them. Or can you?

No pressure, Luna. I get it.

You did hope that one kid gets his PlayStation. That one college student passes his exams. That one couple ends up together and things work out for them. I know you did, and I want you to find comfort in the fact that a tiny speck of biological and chemical arrangement realizes and appreciates your effort.

I like to think that you did indeed get your own authority, if not magic, to make certain things work. You are magical, after all. You are perfect, Luna. What terrible fate would we life forms have had if our planet had no "you"? What would we look up to? What would reassure us at night? I hope you appreciate our countless pages of poetry written for you. Our metaphors for you. Our astrological assumptions and connections made according to you. I know that time moves slow for you, and me, or people alive with me at this age, are nothing but a speck in the grand scale of what is to come, but you are permanent. You will remember. And you are bound to me because of that. Bound to every single person who has thought of you, meditated upon you.

It is a thing of much misfortune that you are leaving Earth. I will not be alive, in this form at least, to witness your departure, and I don't quite know if that is my fortune or otherwise. If humanity still exists then, and has to witness you disappear into nothingness, then it would be one of the greatest tragedies for us, and for the spirit and soul of Earth herself. If I could find my way into such a future in a physical body, yet keep my memories from this time, I would certainly find incredible joy in picking up works related to you. I would gladly compile and work on rediscovering poems and literary works of all sorts ever written about you, make some myself, draw you if they asked me to, and I would charge heavily for it. I hope that if reincarnation is real, then this job, of being a "moonlived," is too, and highly paid. They would have to accept me — without doubt. Talking about death, when I am dead and my soul leaves my body, as Pliny the Elder assumed, then I wish for free travel beyond speed and time, across the planet and beyond it. Maybe this way I would be able to see your other side as well, sit atop your dust, and if I had brought with me some water, then maybe build some... moon-castles? Or just take more photos, so I have real reference when it comes to drawing you for the forsaken, when I become a moonlived. If someone then is still a true human in spirit, I might come with them to you once in a while. All fantasies.

But for now, you're not leaving. I certainly am going to, before you. But that doesn't change anything, because I probably already have... multiple times, all in front of you. In my next life, if I take birth in the past as a dumb Neanderthal who knows only to look but not to reason, I would enjoy simply staring at you with no such thoughts — none at all which poke at me. I can imagine an entirely different sense of awe that could be evoked in a mind that is not bickering with such sentences, questions, or wishes, and has only the task of looking at you. It is beyond absurd how then I could just look up, an unfathomable distance away in the night sky, and look at a glimmering circle. What is it? What does it do? How do I reach it? Who am I kidding — I would just pray to you.

And exactly that is what has never changed. I have, and still do, pray to you. Pray for you, even. We all pray to what is a higher force. Hopefully you pray for us too, and for yourself, to someone... maybe Jupiter? Like man to God for himself, and for his children, and for them to pray to him for their demands.

I only say such things because now that we have been friends for so long, I assume you would be okay with someone talking about these things all up in your face. I do not want to force you to be my friend, so I am a little icky sticking that title on us, but know this — if I was to ever look up at you one random night, and I saw a little corner of you sparkle at me, I will take that as permission, and probably wink back at you. But for now, let us assume that we're just close by the means of this conversation, and this only, regardless of our past. What if you are someone who is scared of direct confrontation? What if you curse me, God forbid? But you probably could not be fazed by something as insignificant as a mound of cylindrical flesh, would you? But I assume, once again, that you like conversations every once in a while, with me, and a random lady talking to you from a house roof in Colmar. Do you see it, Luna? Too many assumptions for both of our likings... maybe knock back once? In this lifetime of mine, if possible, for I do not know if these same thoughts and prayers carry over in a rebirth. If you tried to talk to me when I am a maniac scientist in my basement, I would barely recognize it.

If humanity advances so much that trips to your land become a thing, I apologize in advance. But you can scoff at me for what I am about to say — I would definitely take such a trip, put all my remaining wealth into it if possible. I would be elevated in every way possible if I get to hold you in any of my lives, and if I am a soul of indefinite luck, I may just have the opportunity to die on you. Might have to check my karmic wealth bank for that — I am probably running severely short.

But hey, no stress. Let me know when you are ready for a conversation. I will be waiting. I will. Until then, do not expect me to go quiet. The roof is permanent, so are you, I hope. And oh boy, am I willing to exploit this permanence and throw my speech out there, hoping anything reaches you.

This has been it for tonight. I must go back downstairs. But until then, Luna, I will wait for you. And this has been my patient call to you.

Have a nice morning, Luna.
